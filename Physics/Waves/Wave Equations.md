# Period and Frequency
The Period and Frequency of a wave can be related by the following two equations:
(For the definitions of these quantities, see [[Properties of a wave]].)

$$f = \frac{1}{T}$$
$$T = \frac{1}{f}$$

Where:
- $f$ = frequency (Hz)
- $T$ = period (s)

# Wave Speed Equation
$$v = f \lambda$$

Where:
- $v$ = wave speed (m/s)
- $f$ = frequency (Hz)
- $\lambda$ = wavelength (m)
