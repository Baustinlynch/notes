# Hubble's Law

Hubble's Law states that the velocity at which a galaxy is receding from us is proportional to its distance from us.

$$v = H_0 \times d$$

Where:
- $v$ = recessional velocity (km/s)
- $H_0$ = Hubble's constant (km/s/Mpc)
- $d$ = distance to the galaxy (Mpc)

This relationship provides evidence for the expansion of the universe. The further away a galaxy is, the faster it appears to be moving away from us.
