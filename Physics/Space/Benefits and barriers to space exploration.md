## Benefits

### Technology invented for space that is used by normal people

- Radio and breathing systems
    - Radios and breathing apparatus used by firefighters were developed for space exploration

- Biodegradable environmental cleanup
    - Water cleaning systems used for space can be used for cleaning up oil spills and similar incidents

- Phase change materials
    - Clothing that can control heat better than other materials; releases heat when you are hot and keeps heat in when you are cold

- Memory foam
    - Used for sleeping in space but can be used on earth as well.

- Biro pens
    - Needed a pen that wouldn't leak ink everywhere.

### Other benefits

#### Economic growth
Large amounts of investment by the government in specialist industries can help create technology and industries that would not have developed without large amounts of government investment and buying things without the need for it to be economically viable.

#### Climate and weather monitoring
**Space-based** imaging can help **prevent** natural disasters, monitor the effects of climate change and help predict and detect severe weather. This has a large impact on society, far outweighing the cost of the space program.

#### Scientific discoveries made in space
The science that can be done with a space program is deeply beneficial to the culture and academic sector of a country.

## Barriers

Getting things and people to space is very expensive. Things in space are very far away so it takes **a long time** to get there.
