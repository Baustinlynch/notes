A **Spectra** is the full rainbow spectrum of light. 

Different **elements** produce different wavelengths of light. You can find out what elements are present in a star by the light that is given off because of this. The light that it is produced by a star is presented in a spectrum graph. 
(This is only possible because of the telescopes covered in [[Telescopes]], especially space telescopes which can detect the whole EM spectrum.)
