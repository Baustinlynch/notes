As the orbital distance increases, the orbital period (the time it takes for the object to complete a full orbit) increases, because the further away it is from the Earth, the longer the path it takes.

A good way to remember this is that as the radius of the circle is increased - the orbital altitude - the circumference of the circle is larger, so the time it takes to go the path is longer.
(This explains the orbital periods of the different [[Satellites|satellite]] types.)
