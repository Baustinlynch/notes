
### Optical Telescopes
They detect visible **light**. The bigger the telescope the more and further it can see. Cannot be used in bad weather or during the day since they see with visible light. Can be obtained **relatively cheaply**.

### Radio Telescopes
They detect **radio waves**. Stars and other celestial bodies give out not just light but every part of the electromagnetic spectrum. Radio telescopes are used from the surface of the earth because the **atmosphere does not block** radio waves as much as the other parts of the EM spectrum. Radio telescopes can be used in bad weather since they do not use visible light, but they are very expensive and need to be **very** large.

### Space Telescopes
They can detect **all of the EM spectrum** since it is not blocked by the atmosphere. Can be manoeuvred around space but **are** difficult to launch and maintain. **Very expensive**.
(The different elements in stars give off different wavelengths of light, which is how astronomers use [[Spectra|spectra]] to analyse them.)
