A rocket is travelling to the planet Zorg, which has a $g$ of $5\,\text{ms}^{-2}$. It has no atmosphere. The rocket has $m = 25,000\,\text{kg}$

1. Draw a diagram labelling all the forces as it descends to the planet at a constant speed.
	   ![[Drawing 2026-08-31 09.04.39.excalidraw]]
2. What is the thrust?
   $T=w=mg$
   $=25,000\times5$
   $T=125,000\,\text{N (up)}$
3. The thrust is then increased to $150,000\,\text{N}$, what is the deceleration?
%% col-start %%

%% col-break:b:secondary %%

$F=ma$
$25,000=25,000\times a$
$a=1\,\text{ms}^{-2}$ 

%% col-break:b:secondary %%

$\Delta T=150,000-125,000$  
$\Delta T=25,000=F$

%% col-end %%

4. The rocket was descending at $3\,\text{ms}^{-1}$ when the thrust was increased. It hits the ground at $0.2\,\text{ms}^{-1}$. At what height above the ground was the thrust increased?
   $v^2=u^2+2as$
   $0.2^2=3^2+2\times(-1)\times s$
   $S=4.48\,\text{m}$
