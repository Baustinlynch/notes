## Measuring Acceleration
as part of the Higher course, you must be able to describe an experiment to measure acceleration.

---
To measure the acceleration, we used a light gate and light gate controler
We measured:
- the width of the masks on the car
- the angle of the surface that the car was rolling down
We fed the width of each of the masks into the light gate controler, and it measured the time that it took for the mask to go past the light gate, which allowed it to calculate the speed that the car was moving when each mask went past, it used these measurements to calculate the $\Delta v$, by subtracting the first measurement from the second measurement. It then took the time between each of the masks passing to calculate the acceleration, and we got this data:

| angle | acc1 | acc2 | acc3 |
| ----- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| 6.9   | 1.73 | 2.44 | 2.37 |
| 8.6   | 3.44 | 3.53 | 3.29 |
| 10.2  | 4.8  | 4.45 | 4.59 |
| 12    | 8.86 | 8.57 | 9.52 |
To keep the experiment fair, we kept the surface that the car was rolling down, and the position the light gate was in, and the position that the car was released from the same.
As you can see in our data, we changed the angle of the rolling surface, and the higher the angle, the higher the acceleration on average.
