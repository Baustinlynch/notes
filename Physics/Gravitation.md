 There is a force of attraction between any 2 objects that have mass. The forces depend upon:
- the mass of the objects
- the distance between them
The formula for gravitation is:
$$F=G\frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{r^2}$$
$G$ is the universal gravitational constant (on data sheet). This formula works for spherical objects, e.g. stars and planets
(This is what keeps planets and [[Satellites|satellites]] in their [[Orbits|orbits]].)
### Examples:
1. Find the force exerted on the moon due to the earth.
   $M_{\text{moon}}=7.3\times10^{22}kg$
   $M_{\text{earth}}6\times 10^{24}kg$
   $\text{radius of orbit}=3.84\times 10^8m$
   $G=6.67\times 10^{-11}m^3kg^{-1}s^{-2}$
   $$F=G\frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{r^2}$$
   $$F=6.67\times 10^{-11}\times \frac{7.3\times10^{22}\times 6\times 10^{24}}{(3.84\times 10^8)^2}$$
   $$F= 198'124'186'197'916'666'667= 1.98\times 10^{20}$$
