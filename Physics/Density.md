Density is defined as the **mass per unit volume**:
$$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$$
where $\rho$ (rho) is density, $m$ is mass, and $V$ is volume.

## Examples:
### Aluminium
$m = 111\ \text{g} = 111 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{kg}$
$V_{\text{calculated}} = 99\ \text{mm} \times 20\ \text{mm} \times 20\ \text{mm} = 3.96 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3$
$\rho = \frac{111 \times 10^{-3}}{3.96 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3}$
$\rho = 2,803.03\ \text{kg/m}^3$

$V_{\text{measured}} = 38 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3$
$\rho = \frac{111 \times 10^{-3}}{38 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3}$
$\rho = 2,921.05\ \text{kg/m}^3$

### Marble
$m = 105\ \text{g} = 105 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{kg}$
$V_{\text{calculated}} = 100\ \text{mm} \times 20\ \text{mm} \times 18.5\ \text{mm} = 3.7 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^3$

### Block 3
$m = 129\ \text{g} = 129 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{kg}$
$V_{\text{calculated}} = \dots$
$V_{\text{measured}} = 17\ \text{ml} = 17 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{m}^3$
$\rho = \frac{129 \times 10^{-3}}{17 \times 10^{-6}} = 7,588\ \text{kg/m}^3$
