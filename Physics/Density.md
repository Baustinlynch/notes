Density is defined as the **mass per unit volume** $mass=\frac{mass}{volume}$ 
$$p=\frac{m}{v}$$
## Examples:
### Aluminium
$mass=111g=111\times10^{-3}kg$ 
$Volume(calculated)=99mm\times20mm\times20mm=3.96\times10^{-5}m^3$  
$p=\frac{111\times10^{-3}}{3.96\times10^{-5}m^2}$
$p=2803.03kgm^{3}$

$Volume(measured)=38\times10^{-5}m^3$ 
$p=\frac{111\times10^{-3}}{38\times10^{-5}m^2}$
$p=2,921.05kgm^3$

### Marble
$mass=105g=105\times10^{-3}$
$Volume(calculated)=100mm\times20mm\times18.5mm=3.7\times10^{-5}M^3$
