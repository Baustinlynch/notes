The design principles describe guidelines which designers use to make their work more eye-catching, memorable, usable or readable.
The design principles give us a vocabulary for describing how a graphic design succeeds or fails.

| Contrast   | Emphasis       |
| Depth      | Proportion     |
| Dominance  | Rhythm         |
| Line       | Whitespace     |
| Unity      | Proximity      |
|            | Grid structure |
Design elements and principles make up ~30% of the higher graphics question paper, and ~50% of the assignment
Like CAD they make up much of the knowledge and skills required to succeed at Higher Graphic Communication
# Design Elements
Design elements are, generally, considered to be graphic items on a DTP document which are visible. For example, the rectangular shape that encompasses a piece of text.
QS describes design elements as 'individual and tangible graphic items that are visible'
