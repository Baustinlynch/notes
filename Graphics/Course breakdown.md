# Assignment
36% of the Higher course mark is completed in class time (closed book). A series of tasks allowing you to demonstrate skills in preliminary, production and promotional graphics.

## Course exam
64% of the Higher course mark. Tests understanding of CAD, DTP, creative layout techniques, drawing standards and conventions, visual literacy and impacts on society and the environment. Requires written answers and sketching is encouraged to fully explain answers
