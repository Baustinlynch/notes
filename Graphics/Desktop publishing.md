# CMYK color
Magazines, posters and other high quality printed media use four colours of ink:
Cyan, magenta, yellow, black
Most visible colors can be created by mixing these 4 colors. The inks are printed one after another.
DTP software should be set to display CMYK colours when working on projects that will be printed.

# Text justification
### Left justified
All text is aligned on the left-hand side of the text box

### Right justified
All text is aligned on the right-hand side of the text box.

## Full justified
Text reaches both sides of the text box allowing for neat sharp edges at the side of the text box.

## Centre justified
Text is aligned in the centre; text does not reach the edges

# Visuals
Visuals are better quality representations of the final idea for presentation. They can be manually or digitally produced. The images used at this stage may be of lower quality than the final presentation.
Annotating the visuals as they are produced can help you improve the structure and organisation of the thoughts and processes that naturally take place. It makes it easier to make appropriate improvements and optimise the evolution process.
