The use of colour is important to illustration and graphic design as it makes graphics look more realistic and helps designers create a mood or feeling. Colour also affects the way we feel about and react to everyday things.
Deciding on the most suitable colours for a design is more complex than just choosing your favourite colour.
## Primary, Secondary and Tertiary colours
### Primary colours
These are the three basic colours and cannot be made by mixing other colours together.
*(Image needed: Red, Yellow, Blue)*
### Secondary colours
These are produced by mixing two primary colours together.
*(Image needed: Orange, Green, Violet)*
### Tertiary colours
These are produced by mixing one primary colour and one secondary colour. They take their names from the two mixed colours.
*(Image needed: Tertiary colours)*
