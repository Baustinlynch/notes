# Bottom-Up Modeling

Individual parts are separately modelled from preliminary sketches. Next, the parts are gathered into an assembly.
This approach relies on very accurate parts and a great deal of preliminary design work.
Errors in the parts can be discovered during the assembly, where features might not line up properly. Errors can be time consuming and expensive to fix.
