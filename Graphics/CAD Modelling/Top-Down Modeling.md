# Top-Down Modeling

'Top down' is using dimensions from existing geometry or parts/models to generate new parts or modeling parts within an assembly environment. Features, edges, sketches are shared between parts. Geometry is projected between parts to ensure perfect fits. A change to one component will ripple through and update the whole assembly. This speeds up the process of creating complex assemblies. Individual parts can be exported from the assembly model.
