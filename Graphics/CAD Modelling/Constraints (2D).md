# Constraints (2D)

## Parallel
When you constrain two lines parallel to each other.

## Perpendicular
When you constrain two lines at a right angle to each other.

## Answering a constraint question

When answering a constraint question you should ensure you use the correct terminology:
- Constrain - Align/Mate/Center Axis
- Part - Face/Center axis

Like any other 3D modeling question, use sketches to help explain.
Use arrows and 'colour in' areas to help explain how you would constrain parts together.
Each part usually has a **minimum** of two constraints.
