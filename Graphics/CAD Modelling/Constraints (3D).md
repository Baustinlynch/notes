# Constraints (3D)

## Mate
Attaches 2 flat surfaces.
They will still be able to move to the side/backwards and forwards until you secure them in place by adding further constraints.

## Center Axis
Used to connect cylindrical objects - used to insert them or connect holes.
Select the center axis. This will line up the centers, but you will need further constraints, as they will still be able to move linearly along the axis.

## Align
Used to make two flat surfaces sit level with each other.
The parts will still move side to side/backwards and forwards, but will stay parallel to each other. Therefore you must secure it in place with further constraints.

## Tangent
Used to connect 2 cylindrical objects side by side by their tangent. Select the circumference from the models and they will then connect. This usually needs further constraining to secure the cylinders.

## Concentric
When you constrain two or more circles to the same center axis.
