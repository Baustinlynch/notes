# Extra Constraint Knowledge

## Offset
**Offset** can be used to constrain parts but move them into a specific position.
For example, this phone was **mate-constrained** on the desk (1). The side of the phone would then be **aligned** to the side of the desk (2). This lines them up, therefore **offset** was then used to push the phone into position (3).

![](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0b407-80fc-72ff-9cff-c838c00172a1/Constraints1.png)

## Workplanes
Workplanes can be used to help constrain, especially cylindrical objects.
For example, the toothpaste tube lid was not facing the correct way (the pink parts should both be at the front). Work planes through the center of both parts are turned on. Work planes are then mated to line up the parts and secure them.
