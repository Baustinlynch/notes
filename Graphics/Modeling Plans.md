A modeling plan is a step-by-step description of how to make a part in CAD. It has formal language and uses words demanded by the SQA. Words are accompanied by sketches of **profiles** and **paths**. These sketches should be **fully dimensioned** to British Standards.

#### Tips and traps
You must have a clear idea of how to CAD-model a part before writing a modelling plan.
Be careful where you insert a **shell** command; the order is important.
Use **add** or **subtract**, never 'cut' or 'remove'
**Radial arrays** and **linear arrays**, never 'patterns' or 'duplicates'

	- Extrude along a path
