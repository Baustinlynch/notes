## Orthographics
Ann orthographic is a production drawing.
It shows different views of a product and has the dimensions necessary for manufacture
They are drawn in [[Third angle projection]]
### Layout of an orthographic
An orthographic drawing is in in 3 sections, the **plan**, **elevation** and **end elevation**.
The **plan** shows the top surface of the product, it is projected from the elevation. The **elevation** is the front view of the part, and the **end elevation** is the side view of the product.
