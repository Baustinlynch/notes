## Orthographic
A 2D drawing that shows different views/sides of an object, often used for manufacturing.
![](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a10c4e-348a-766b-b169-94a3bba3d47d/image.png)
### How to identify
- Includes dimensions
- Drawn in 3rd angle projection
- Usually 2 or 3 related views - Elevation, plan, end elevation, sectional elevation/plan/end elevation
- Includes hidden detail, dimensions, cutting planes and outlines
- Includes title block

## Assembly
A 2D drawing that shows components assembled with others.
**Sub-assembly**
A sub-assembly is when components create an assembly that is used within another assembly.

![](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a10c53-193b-72df-97e7-d99763ad5edb/image.png)

### How to identify
- Multiple components
- In 3rd angle projection
- No dimensions or hidden detail (usually)
