## Section 1 - Poetry Text 4

49. - "My mum is on a high bed next to the sad chrysanthemums" 
    - the imagery of the high bed makes the reader think that Kay's mum is in hospital, as hospital beds are high.
    - "i am scared my mum is going to die
      on the bed next to the sad chrysanthemums"
    - The chrysanthemums are personified as being sad which connotes how they have been left to degrade and sets the tone of the room with kay's mother as sad.
