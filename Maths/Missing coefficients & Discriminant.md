
#### Examples
1. Find $p$ given that $x^2+(p+1)x+9=0$ has *equal roots*
	$b^2-4ac=0$ for equal roots;
	$a=1$, $b=p+1$, $c=9$
	$(p+1)^2-4\times 1\times 9=0$
	$p^2+2p+1-36=0$
	$p^2+2p-35=0$
	$(p+7)(p+5)=0$
	$p=-7$, $p=5$

	or, we can keep the squared bracket squared
	$(p+1)^2-36=0$
	$p+1=\pm 6$
	$p+1=6$  $p+1=-6$
	$p=5$  $p=-7$

2. For what  values of $m$ does the equation $x^2-2x+m=0$ have **real roots**
   $b^2-4ac\ge0$
   $(-2)^2-4\times 1\times m\ge0$
   $4-4m\ge0$
   $-4m\ge-4$
   $m\le1$ 
   ==🟡(remember to flip the sign)==

3. Show that the roots of the equation $(x-2)(x-3)=k^2$ are always real
   $x^2-5x+6-k^2=0$                $b^2-4ac\ge 0$
   $a-1$, $b=-5$, $c=6-k^2$
   $(-5)^2-4\times 1(6-k^2)$
   $=25-4(6-k^2)$
   $=25-24+4k^2$
   $=1+4k^2$
   $k^2\ge 0$ for all values of $k$
   $4k^2\ge 0$ $\forall k$
   $1+4k^2\ge 0$ $\forall k$
   $b^2-4ac\ge 0 ~\forall k$ so the roots are always real
