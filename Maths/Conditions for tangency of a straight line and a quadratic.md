1. Show that $y=2x+7$ is tangent to the curve $y=6-x^2$ and find the coordinates of the point of contact.
   The line and the curve meet where $2x+7=6-x^2$, $\therefore x^2+2x+1=0$, and $a=1$, $b=2$, $c=1$
   $b^2-4ac=2^2-4(1)(1)=0 \therefore$ one point of contact, hence the line is a tangent.
   $x^2+2x+1=0$
   $(x+1)(x+1)=0$
   $\therefore x=-1$
   $y=2(-1)+7=5$
   Point of intersection is (-1,5)

2. A tangent to the parabola $y=x^2-4x$ has a gradient of 2.
	   ==🟠(a)Find the equation of the tangent==
	   If m= ==🟡2==, then $y=2x+c$
	   Since the line is a tangent,
	   $x^2-4x=2x+c$
	   $x^2-6x-c=0$, and $b-4ac=0$
	   ==🔴a=1, b=-6, c=-c==
	   $(-6)^2-4(1)(-c)=0$
	   $36+4c=0$
	   $4c=-36$
	   $c=-9$
	   $\therefore \text{the equation is }y=2x-9$
	   ==🟠(b) Find the point of contact==
	   $x^2-4x=2x-9$
	   $x^2-6x+9=0$
	   $(x-3)(x-3)=0$
	   $\therefore x=3$
	   $y=2(3)-9=-3$
	   Point of contact is <u>$(3,-3)$</u>

3. Find the equations of the tangent from (0,-1) to $y=x^2-4x+3$.
   All lines that pass through (0,-1) are of the form $y=mx-1$.
   $x^2-4+3=mx-1$
   $x^2-3x-mx+4=0$
   $x^2+x(-4-m)+4=0$
   Tangency implies equal roots, therefore $b^2-4ac=0$
   $a=1$, $b=(-4-m)$, $c=4$
   $(-4-m)^2-4(1)(4)=0$
   $16+8m=0$
   $m^2+8m$
   $m(m+8)$
   **Equations:**
   $m=0$
   <u>$y=-1$</u>
   
   $m=-8$
   y=-8x-1$
