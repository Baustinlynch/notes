[[Exponential Function|Exponential Functions]] have the form:
$$f(x)=a^x~~~~~~~~~x \in\mathbb{R},a\in\mathbb{R},a>0$$
This is an [[Exponential Function]] to base $a$.

### Graphing an exponential function

| $X$        | -3        | -2        | -1        | 0   | 1   | 2   | 3   |
| ---------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $f(x)=2^x$ | $\frac18$ | $\frac14$ | $\frac12$ | 1   | 2   | 4   | 8   |

> [!NOTE]  Important features!
> The values never go into the negative!
> The graph will never touch the x-axis!
> An exponential graph with the equation $y=a^x$ will **always** go through the point (0,1).
> Exponential graphs will **always** go through the point (1,$a$), where $a$ is the base.
