# Differentiation - Derivative of Products

Remember we often have to rewrite $f(x)$ before differentiating. We have to rewrite products by multiplying out.

### Examples
1. $$f(x)=(x+3)(x+3) = x^2+6x+9$$
   $$f'(x)=2x+6$$
2. $$f(x)=x^{1/2}(x^{3/2}+x^{1/2}) = x^2+x$$
   $$f'(x)=2x+1$$
3. $$f(x)=\sqrt{x}\left( 2x-\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \right) = 2x^{3/2}-1$$
   $$f'(x)=3x^{1/2}$$
