# Differentiation - Chain Rule (Composite Functions)

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\times \frac{du}{dx}$$

### Examples
1. $y=(5x^3-3x^2)^7$
   Let $u=5x^3-3x^2$, then $\frac{du}{dx}=15x^2-6x$
   $y=u^7$, then $\frac{dy}{du}=7u^6$
   $$\frac{dy}{dx}=7(5x^3-3x^2)^6 \times (15x^2-6x)$$
