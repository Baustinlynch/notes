# Differentiation - Derivatives in Context

The distance $d$, in meters, covered by a model train engine is given by $d(t)=1-4t+t^2~~~~t \geq 0$
Where $t$ is the time in seconds after the engine started to move.
Calculate the speed of the engine 5 seconds after the start.
$$\text{Distance} = f(t) \to \text{speed} = f'(t)$$
$$\text{speed}=d'(t)$$
$$d'(t)=-4+2t$$
$$d'(5)=-4+2\times 5 = 6\text{ms}^{-1}$$
