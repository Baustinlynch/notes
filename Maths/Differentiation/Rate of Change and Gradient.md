# Differentiation - Rate of Change and Gradient

### Rate of change/gradient of the tangent
1. Find the gradient of the tangent of $f(x)$ when $x=2$ if 
   $$f(x)=x^2+\frac{1}{x}$$
   $$f(x)=x^2+x^{-1}$$
   $$f'(x)=2x-x^{-2}$$
   $$f'(x)=2x-\frac{1}{x^2}$$
   $$f'(2)=2\times 2-\frac{1}{2^2} = 4-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{15}{4}$$
2. Find the rate of change of $D(t)=t^3-t^2$ when $t=10$
   $$D(t)=t^3-t^2$$
   $$D'(t)=3t^2-2t$$
   $$D'(10)=3\times 10^2-2\times 10 = 300-20 = 280$$
