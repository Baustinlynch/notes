# Differentiation - Derivative of Quotients/Fractions

We also have to rewrite quotients (expressions with fractions) before differentiating.

### Examples
Differentiate the following functions:
1. $f(x)=\frac{x^2-2x+1}{x} = x-2+x^{-1}$
   $$f'(x)=1-x^{-2}$$
2. $f(x)=\frac{2x+3}{\sqrt{x}} = 2x^{1/2}+3x^{-1/2}$
   $$f'(x)=x^{-1/2}-\frac{3}{2}x^{-3/2}$$
