# Differentiation - Differentiable Form

To be differentiable, $f(x)$ must be written in [[Indices|index form]].

### Example
Differentiate the following:
1. $f(x)=\frac{1}{x^3}=x^{-3}$
   $f'(x)=-3x^{-4}$
2. $f(x)=\sqrt{x}=x^{1/2}$
   $f'(x)=\frac{1}{2}x^{-1/2}$
3. $f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{x}} = x^{-1/3}$
   $f'(x)=-\frac{1}{3}x^{-4/3}$
