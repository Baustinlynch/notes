# Differentiation - Leibniz Notation

Another way of writing the derivative is: (linked to the [[Distance formula|gradient between two points]])
$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{\text{difference in y}}{\text{difference in x}}=\frac{\text{y-step}}{\text{x-step}}=\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$$
$$\frac{dy}{dx}=f'(x)$$

### Examples
1. Find the gradient of $y=x^3-5x^2+3x$ at $(2,-6)$
   $$\frac{dy}{dx}=3x^2-10x+3$$
   at $x=2$:
   $$\frac{dy}{dx}=3(2)^2-10(2)+3 = 12-20+3 = -5$$
2. If $y=t^3-3t^{-2}$ find the derivative of $y$ with respect to $t$
   $$\frac{dy}{dt}=3t^2+6t^{-3}$$
