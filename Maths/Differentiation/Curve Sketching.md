# Differentiation - Curve Sketching

### Steps
1. Find the points where the curve crosses the axes
   - y-axis — where $x=0$ find $f(0)$
   - x-axis — where $y=0$ solve $f(x)=0$
2. Find the stationary points and their nature
   - solve $f'(x)=0$
   - check $\pm$ of $f'(x)$ on either side of SPs
3. Confirm basic shape of curve
   - look at behaviour of $f(x)$ as $x\to \pm \infty$
