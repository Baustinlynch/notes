# Differentiation - Derivative of ax^n

> [!NOTE] Rule
> If $f(x)=ax^n$
> then $f'(x)=anx^{n-1}$

### Examples
Differentiate:
1. $f(x)=2x^3$
   $f'(x)=2\times 3x^2 = 6x^2$
2. $f(x)=\frac{6}{x^3}=6x^{-3}$
   $f'(x)=-18x^{-4} = -\frac{18}{x^4}$
3. $f(x)=\frac{1}{2x^4} = \frac{1}{2}x^{-4}$
   $f'(x)=-2x^{-5}$
