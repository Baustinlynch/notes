# Differentiation - Equation of a Tangent

### Example
Find the equation of the tangent at $x=16$ for $f(x)=\sqrt{x^3}=x^{3/2}$

**Gradient**
$$f'(x)=\frac{3}{2}x^{1/2}$$
$$f'(16)=\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{16}=\frac{3}{2}\times 4 = 6$$
$$m=6$$

**Equation**
$$f(16)=\sqrt{16^3}=4^3=64$$
$$(a,b) = (16,64)$$
$$y-b=m(x-a)$$
$$y-64=6(x-16)$$
$$y=6x-32$$
