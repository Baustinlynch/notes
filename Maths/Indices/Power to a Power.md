# Indices - Power to a Power

## Raising a power to a power
$$(a^n)^m=a^{n\times m}$$

### Examples: Simplify by writing in index form
1. $(x^4)^6=x^{24}$
2. $(y^2)^{-3}=y^{-6}$
3. $(2x^4)^3=2^3x^{12}=8x^{12}$
4. $(x^3y^5)^4=x^{12}y^{20}$
