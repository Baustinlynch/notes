# Indices - Dividing Powers

## Dividing powers of the same base
For any whole numbers $n,m$ (positive or negative)
$$\frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$$

### Examples: Simplify by writing in index form
1. $\frac{x^6}{x^4}=x^2$
2. $\frac{8a^7}{2a^2}=\frac{8}{2}\times \frac{a^7}{a^2}=4a^5$
3. $\frac{2b^3\times 3b^4}{4b^2}=\frac{6b^7}{4b^2}=\frac{3}{2}b^5$
