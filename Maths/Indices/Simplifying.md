# Indices - Simplifying with Positive Indices

### Simplify and express with a positive index
1. $x^{\frac{1}{2}}\times x=x^{\frac{1}{2}+1}=x^{\frac{3}{2}}$
2. $5a^{\frac{2}{3}}\times 3a^{-\frac{1}{3}}=15a^{\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{3}}=15a^{\frac{1}{3}}$
