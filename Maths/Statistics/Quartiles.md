# Statistics - Quartiles

The **Median** divides a set of data into **two parts**: the **lower half** and the **upper half**.

The **lower quartile (Q1)** is the median of the lower half.
The **upper quartile (Q3)** is the median of the upper half.
**Q2** is the median of the whole data set.

> [!NOTE]
> The **Quartiles** divide a dataset into quarters - hence the word **Quart**ile.

### Examples
1. 1  2  3 **|** 4  5  5  **6**  7  7  8 **|** 8  9  10
      **Q1**          **Q2**          **Q3**
   Q2 = Median = 7th value = 6
   Q1 = 3.5
   Q3 = 8

2. 8  9  10 **|** 10  11  12 **|** 13  13  14 **|** 16 16 18
   Q2 = Median = Between 6th and 7th value = 12.5
   Q1 = 10
   Q3 = 15
