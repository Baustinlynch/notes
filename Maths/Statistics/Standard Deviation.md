# Statistics - Standard Deviation

The standard deviation of a data set is another way to **measure the spread of a data set.**
It calculates **how much, on average**, the data values **deviate from the mean**.

$$S=\sqrt{\frac{\Sigma(x-\overline{x})^2}{n-1}}$$
$$\Sigma=\text{Sum of}$$
$$S=\sqrt{\frac{\text{Sum of }(x-\overline{x})^2}{\text{Number of values}-1}}$$

### Without the Formula
Calculate the standard deviation of: 4, 9, 10, 12, 15

1. Mean $\overline{x} = \frac{50}{5} = 10$
2. Table:
   | $x$ | $x-\overline{x}$ | $(x-\overline{x})^2$ |
   | --- | ---------------- | -------------------- |
   | 4   | -6               | 36                   |
   | 9   | -1               | 1                    |
   | 10  | 0                | 0                    |
   | 12  | 2                | 4                    |
   | 15  | 5                | 25                   |
   |     | Sum =            | 66                   |
3. $S = \sqrt{\frac{66}{4}} = \sqrt{16.5} = 4.062$ (3dp)

### With the Formula
A runner recorded her time in seconds for 6 laps: 55, 57, 58, 60, 55, 56

1. $\overline{x} = \frac{341}{6} = 56.83\text{s}$
2. Table:
   | $x$ | $x-\overline{x}$ | $(x-\overline{x})^2$ |
   | --- | ---------------- | -------------------- |
   | 55  | -1.8             | 3.24                 |
   | 57  | 0.2              | 0.04                 |
   | 58  | 1.2              | 1.44                 |
   | 60  | 3.2              | 10.24                |
   | 55  | -1.8             | 3.24                 |
   | 56  | -0.8             | 0.64                 |
   |     | Sum =            | 18.84                |
3. $S = \sqrt{\frac{18.84}{5}} = \sqrt{3.768} = 1.94\text{s}$

### Comparison Example
After changing her routine: mean = 55s, standard deviation = 3.2s

**Before:** $\overline{x} = 56.8$s, $S = 1.94$s
**After:** $\overline{x} = 55$s, $S = 3.2$s

1. After the change she went faster on average
2. After the change her running times were less consistent
