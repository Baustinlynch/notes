# Statistics - Interquartile Range

**Interquartile range** = Q3 - Q1

The IQR describes the range/how spread out the **middle 50% of the data set** is.
The **smaller the IQR** the **more similar or consistent** the middle 50% of the data set is.

### Example
The weights of a group of 15-year-old boys was recorded in kg:
60  60  62 **|** 64  65  65 **|** 66  69  70 **|** 71  72  83

$Q1=63$
$Q2 = 65.5$
$Q3 = 70.5$
$$IQR=70.5-63 = 7.5$$

### Comparing Two Groups
The weights of twelve 14-year-old boys was also recorded in kg. 
For this group: Median=61kg, IQR=9kg

**15 yrs:** Median = 65.5, IQR = 7.5
**14 yrs:** Median = 61, IQR = 9

Two ways to compare:
1. **Using the Medians**: On average, the weight of 15 year old boys is higher than the weight of 14 year olds.
2. **Using the IQR**: On average, the weights of 15 year old boys are **more consistent** than the weights of 14 year old boys.
