There are 2 main ways to solve a quadratic equation, through **factorising**, and through **the quadratic formula**.

One of the easiest ways to solve a quadratic equation/find the roots of the quadratic graph is to **factorise the quadratic equation**. e.g.
$x^2+4x+3=0$
$(x+1)(x+3)=0$
$\underline{x=-1}$ or $\underline{x=-3}$

Some slightly more complicated equations require you to rearrange the equation before you can factorise. For example,
$x^2+11x+34=4$
$x^2+11x+34-4=4-4$
$x^2+11x+30=0$
$(x+5)(x+6)=0$
$\underline{x=-5}$ or $\underline{x=-6}$

To solve more complicated quadratic equations, you use the **quadratic formula**, $x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$. (For simpler quadratics, especially those without linear terms, see [[Solving quadratic equations]].) For example, to solve the equation $2x^2+6x+3=0$ you first need to find the coefficients $a$, $b$ and $c$; you do this by putting the equation into the form of the quadratic equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$. In our case, the equation is already in the same form, so our coefficients are $a=2$, $b=6$ and $c=3$. Next substitute our coefficients into the quadratic formula, $x=\frac{-6\pm\sqrt{6^2-4\times2\times3}}{2\times2}$. Once you have substituted the coefficients, you just need to solve the equation:
$x=\frac{-6\pm\sqrt{6^2-4\times2\times3}}{2\times2}$
$x=\frac{-6\pm\sqrt{12}}{4}$
$x=\frac{-6+\sqrt{12}}{4}$ or $x=\frac{-6-\sqrt{12}}{4}$
$\underline{x\approx -0.634}$ or $\underline{x\approx -2.366}$

## Finding the axis of symmetry of a quadratic graph
When a graph is in the form of the quadratic equation, you can use the formula $x=-\frac{b}{2a}$. For example:
For the equation $y=x^2-4x+3$, $a=1$ and $b=-4$, so:
$x=-\frac{-4}{2\times1}$
$\underline{x=2}$

**Or** you can use the roots of the graph to find the axis of symmetry. Since the axis of symmetry is the centre of the roots, if you find the point between the roots then you can find the axis of symmetry. For example,
If the roots of an equation are -1 and -3, then the equation of the axis of symmetry will be $x=-2$.
