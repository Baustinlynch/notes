Points on the same line are **collinear**
![[Collinearity-diagram.excalidraw]]
If points A, B, C are collinear then $m_{AB}=m_{BC}$ and lines AB and BC share a common point, B.

#### Example:
Show that X(-6,1), Y(2,-7), Z(-2,-3) are collinear
$m_{XY}=\frac{-7-1}{2-(-6)}=\frac{-8}{8}=-1$
$m_{YZ}=\frac{-3-(-7)}{-2-2}=\frac{4}{-4}=-1$
For these points, $m_{XY}=m_{YZ}$ so XY and YZ are parallel and share a common point, Y.
Therefore X, Y and Z are collinear.
