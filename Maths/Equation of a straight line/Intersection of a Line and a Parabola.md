Where does the graph of $y=x^2+3x+2$ intersect with the graph of $y=x+5$?
![[Drawing 2026-11-02 12.17.55.excalidraw]]
==🔴**Equate together**==:
$x^2+3x+2=x+5$
$x^2+2x-3=0$
$(x+3)(x-1)=0$
$x=-3$, $x=1$

==🟠**Find the y-values**== by substituting into the equation:
$y=-3+5$
$y=2$
$(-3,2)$
**or**
$y=1+5$
$y=6$
$(1,6)$

**Ans**:
(Remember to clearly state the coordinates at the end of  the question)

$(-3,2)$, $(1,6)$
