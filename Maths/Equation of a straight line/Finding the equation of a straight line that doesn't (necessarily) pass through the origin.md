The equation of a straight line graph is: $y=mx+c$, where $m$ is the **gradient** and $c$ is the **y-intercept** (where the line **intercepts** the y-axis)
## Example
```desmos-graph
y=3x-1
```
$$y=mx+c$$
$$m=\frac{\text{y-step}}{\text{x-step}}$$
$$m=\frac{3}{1}$$
$$m=3$$
$$c=-1$$
$$y=3x+-1$$
$$y=3x-1$$

(For lines through the origin, see [[Finding the equation of a straight line through the origin]].)
