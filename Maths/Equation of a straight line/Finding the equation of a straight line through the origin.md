For any point (x,y) on a straight line passing through (0,0):
$$y=mx$$
Where $m$ is the **gradient** of the line

## Example:
```desmos-graph
y=-2x
```
a. *Find the equation of this straight line*
$$y=mx$$
$$m=~?$$
$$m=\frac{\text{y-step}}{\text{x-step}}$$
$$m=\frac{-2}{1}$$
$$m=-2$$
$$y=-2x$$
b. *Use the equation to verify that the point (9, -18) is on the line*
$$y=mx$$
$$-2x=-2\times9=-18=y$$

(For lines that do not pass through the origin, see [[Finding the equation of a straight line that doesn't (necessarily) pass through the origin.]].)
