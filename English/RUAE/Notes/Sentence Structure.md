## Types of Sentence Structure
- ***Short/Minor sentences*** - A sentence without a verb
- *Long sentences* - A sentence with lots of clauses, with lots of commas, and lots of bits.
- *Sentence types*; Exclamation/command/statement/question(rhetorical)
- *Repetition* - clause, phrase, word, sound
- Contrast and juxtaposition - used to emphasise the difference
- *Antithesis/ balance*
- ***lists***
- *inversion* - change the word-order of the sentence to improper, like Yoda talk
- *Climax/anti-climax*
- *parenthesis* - add extra information to a sentence — say with an em-dash or brackets — without breaking the flow

## Punctuation
- **!** - can be used to convey tone and emotion
- **:** - can be used to introduce a list
- **;** - to connect 2 independent clauses that are related but could stand equally on their own
- **...** - can be used at the end of a sentence to add suspense and ...
- **-  -** - can be used as parenthesis 
- **( )** - can be used as parenthesis
- **'** - can be used to show omissions in contractions, and can be used to indicate possession, e.g. Mr Williams' pen or Brenden's pen
