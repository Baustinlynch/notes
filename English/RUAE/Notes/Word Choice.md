Word choice is when a writer uses a particular word or phrase in their work to influence the reader and create a particular effect in the mind of the reader.

### Nuance
A subtle difference or very slight difference in meaning, feeling or tone.

"I don't like you" vs "I loath you"

### Sound
Onomatopoeia, alliteration, soft/harsh sounding

## Our Strategy
1. Quote.
2. **Analyse the word in detail —the connotation, associations the word has, sound or tone etc.**
3. **Discuss the effect of the word — make sure that your answer links to the question.**
