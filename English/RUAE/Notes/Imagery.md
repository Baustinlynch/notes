### **Types of imagery:**
- Metaphors
- Similes
- Personification
- Transferred epithet — where an adjective or epithet grammatically qualifies a noun other than the person or thing it is actually describing.
    - e.g. Three out of five forest fires are caused by a careless cigarette.
      The cigarette is not the one who is careless; it is the person who dropped it who was careless.
- Symbolism
- Synecdoche
- Oxymoron
- Synesthesia — when one sense is described by another.


### 1 or 2 mark answers
- Always quote the image and explain the literal meaning, then explain the comparison and its effect.
