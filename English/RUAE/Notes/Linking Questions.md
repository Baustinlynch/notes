Linking questions are **always** worth 2 marks in the exam.
They don't always come up in the exam.
### How to identify
- Writer's structure of argument
- "link"
### How to answer
**First mark** — naming the previous topic **and** quoting the part of the link sentence which relates *back* to it
**Second mark** — naming the topic about to be discussed **and** quoting the part of the link sentence which relates *forward* to it
