Own words questions are questions that ask you to find info in the passage and then say the same thing, but using your own words as much as possible.
At higher, you get **1 mark per point**

## How to answer:
- Find anything you can use in the passage and **underline it**.
- From what you have underlined, take **JUST** enough to answer the question, and translate it.
