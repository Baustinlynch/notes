# Isolation and Loneliness
### Key ideas
- Both characters experience loneliness, but in radically different forms.
- The narrator's loneliness is **modern**: quiet, numbing, shaped by routine and avoidance
- Mal's loneliness is **catastrophic**: everyone he knew is dead.
- Loneliness becomes a **driving force** in the story 
- This theme links to [[Andrina/Theme|Andrina]], where loneliness is eased by another's presence. 

## Loss of community
### Key idea
- Mal's entire village has been wiped out, leaving him as the **sole survivor**.
- Loss of community shapes his identity, his emotional world, and his actions.
- The narrator's modern life contrasts sharply: she has *people around her*, yet feels emotionally isolated
- The story explores how community can shape identity 

## Modern Comfort vs. Ancient Trauma
### Key ideas
- The narrator's life is cosy, safe, and familiar — blankets, phones

## History as an echo
### Key ideas
- Mal's story echoes into the narrator's modern life
- The past is not dead — it reverberates through memory, trauma, and storytelling
- The narrator becomes a witness to history she cannot fully understand
