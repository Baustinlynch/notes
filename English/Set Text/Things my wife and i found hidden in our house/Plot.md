The story uses a **vignette structure**: Short sections, each named after an object in the story.
This creates;
- **Focus** — each object becomes a moment of discovery
- **Escalation** — danger increases step by step
- **Reader involvement** — we connect clues across sections
**Order of Escalation**: Ring -> Paper -> Horse -> Pearls -> Hair -> Jar -> Knife -> More
The story has a rising threat throughout;
- **Ring** — hints at the first wife
- **Paper** — gran's fear ("KELPIES TO HELL")
- **Horse** — burns Alice
- **Pearls** — kelpie bridle
- **Hair** — physical attack
- **Liver** — evidence of kelpie feeding
- **Knife** — silver weapon to kill kelpie
- **More** — the hair, and suggesting that more threats are to come

The story is very linked to Scottish folklore;
- Kelpie = shape-shifting water spirit
	- Associated with drowning, deception, and captivity
	- Logan blends **traditional myth** with **modern domestic life** 
This creates tension between;
- past and present

**Ambiguity & Reader interpretation**
The ending refuses to give the reader closure;
- The couple believes that they have "cleansed" the house
