1. "always gave his mother a cup of tea in bed every morning" 
   The word choice of every shows how Jack is dedicated to caring for his mother, enough that he makes sure to go to see her in the morning without fault to care for her.
   "'try to take a wee sip'... he held the cup to his mothers mouth"
   This quote shows how jack is very caring as he insists that his mother tries to drink the tea, and physically helps her to by bring the mug to her face.
2. "you cannae dee an leave me masel!"
   This quote shows how jack is desperate at the and deeply sad about his mothers mortality, and feels that he cannot cope in life without her
   "I've nae freends, nae naebody in this worl but you.
   This quote shows how jack relies on his mother to ground him, and give him a purpose in life. He dedicates his time to caring for her and wouldn't have anything to do if she was to die
3. "I cannae go on for ever,"
   Jacks mother has accepted her own mortality, and is at peace with the fact that she is going to die. This contrasts with further on in the story, when jack tries to save his mother from death by beating him then trapping him in a nut

(This builds on the themes of responsibility and love in [[Death in a nut/Theme|Death in a Nut]].)
