"It's just common sense." That's what New York State governor Kathy Hochul said when she announced a bill that she claims will be a one-stop way to rid the state of the scourge of 3D-printed ghost guns, and make New York safer for everyone. But is it even possible to implement, let alone enforce this law anywhere in the world, especially a state as populous and dense as New York?

One of the biggest and most glaringly obvious issues with this bill is, how on earth do you implement it? No seriously, I want you to have a think about how you would go about implementing the addition of "gun blocking software" on every 3D printer sold in the state of New York.
