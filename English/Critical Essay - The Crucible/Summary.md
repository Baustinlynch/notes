## Act 4
**At the start of Act 4**
- Houses and farms are abandoned and cows roam free.
- The jails are overflowing with good people.
- **Hope is being lost in Salem**.

1. The act starts with Tituba and Sarah Good rotting away in a Salem jail cell, confessing their dealings with the Devil. They have gone crazy.
2. Parris begs Danforth to postpone the hangings after realising the whole town is in emotional and physical ruin, and wants more time to convince people to lie to save themselves.
3. Hale returns after preaching in Andover about the irrational hysteria of Salem.
4. Elizabeth is brought in to try to get a confession out of John. They are both silent.
5. John confesses to seeing the Devil, but refuses to put his name down out of fear for his family's reputation; he tears up the confession.
