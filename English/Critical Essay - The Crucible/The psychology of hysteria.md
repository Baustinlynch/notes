## Contagion theory
- Crowds and other large groups of people develop a '**group mind**' where emotions spread like they are contagious.
- Anonymity reduces personal responsibility.
- Contagion spreads **fear, anger, or excitement**.

## Mob mentality
Throughout the court proceedings, the town was acting with a mob mentality, but it starts to break down at the start of Act 4, when it is discovered that Abigail and Mercy Lewis have left the town and stolen 30 pounds from Parris. This is the main contention in Act 4, as Hale — who has lost almost all faith in Danforth, and the use of spectral evidence in court proceedings, and maybe has even lost faith in the existence of witches at all — tries to convince the prisoners to lie and admit to being witches to save themselves. Even Parris has started to doubt Danforth and started to argue against Danforth with Hale, and convinces Danforth to bring Elizabeth Proctor to John Proctor and allow her and Hale to try to convince him to admit to being a witch to save himself.
